# ACADEMIC BLACK BOX

> **SEE THE RISK. UNDERSTAND WHY. CHANGE THE OUTCOME.**

**Academic Black Box** is an AI-powered academic early-warning system that helps students understand their academic risk before the semester ends.

Instead of simply saying *"you're at risk,"* the system shows **why the model reached that result**, lets students test hypothetical changes to their semester, and generates an actionable recovery plan.

Built for **WolfHacks 2026 · Institute for Advanced Analytics Track**.

---

## THE IDEA

Academic risk models are often a black box.

A student might receive a prediction without understanding:

- What factors influenced it?
- What part of the decision tree did they fall into?
- Would changing their study habits affect the model's prediction?
- What can they actually do next?

**Academic Black Box turns the prediction into an interactive conversation.**
